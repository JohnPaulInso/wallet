# 🚀 AI Summary Feature - Quick User Guide

## How to Use the Claude-Style Summary Feature

### Method 1: Click the Summary Chip
1. Open SmartWallet AI chatbot
2. Click the **"Complete Summary"** chip (with 📊 icon)
3. Follow the button prompts

### Method 2: Type Keywords
Simply type any of these in the chat:
- `summary`
- `summarize`
- `give me a summary`
- `show summary`
- `complete summary`

---

## Step-by-Step Flow

### Step 1: Choose Summary Type
AI asks: **"What kind of summary would you like?"**

Your options:
- **💰 Expenses Summary** - Total spending, top categories, merchants, trends
- **📊 Category Breakdown** - Spending by category with percentages
- **🎯 Needs/Wants/Savings** - Budget analysis with recommendations
- **🎁 Goals Progress** - Savings goals status and projections
- **🏪 Specific Merchant** - Deep dive into one merchant

### Step 2: Select Time Range
AI asks: **"What time range?"**

Your options:
- **📅 This Month** - Current month only
- **🗓️ June** - June 2026
- **🗓️ May** - May 2026
- **📆 Last 3 Months** - Past 3 months
- **📆 Last 6 Months** - Past 6 months
- **📆 This Year** - January to now
- **📆 All Time** - Complete history

### Step 3: Get Your Summary
- AI generates a detailed, personalized summary
- Includes real transaction data
- Provides actionable recommendations

---

## Special Features

### 🛑 Stop Button
While AI is generating a response:
- The **Send** button transforms into a red **Stop** button
- Click it to abort the AI response mid-generation
- Useful if you want to ask something else quickly

### 🏪 Specific Merchant Flow
When you select "Specific Merchant":
1. AI asks: "Which merchant would you like to see?"
2. Chat input appears
3. Type the merchant name (e.g., "Shopee", "7-Eleven")
4. AI then asks for time range
5. Generates merchant-specific analysis

---

## What Gets Excluded

The AI automatically excludes:
- ❌ **Atome payments** (credit repayments)
- ❌ **Income transactions**

These don't count toward your expense totals or summaries.

---

## Rate Limits

- **50 requests per day** (resets at midnight)
- When limit reached: Professional message with reset time
- Example: "Thank you for using SmartWallet AI! You've reached your daily request limit. Please come back again tomorrow to continue our conversation."

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific with merchants**: Type the exact name as it appears in transactions
2. **Use time ranges wisely**: 
   - This Month → Quick current snapshot
   - Last 3 Months → Trend analysis
   - All Time → Big picture view
3. **Stop if needed**: Don't hesitate to stop and rephrase your question
4. **Follow-up questions**: After getting a summary, ask follow-ups like:
   - "Why is Food & Drinks so high?"
   - "How can I reduce my Shopee spending?"
   - "Am I spending more than last month?"

## Troubleshooting

### AI doesn't detect "summary" keyword
- Make sure you spelled it correctly
- Try other keywords: "summarize", "give me a summary"
- Or just click the "Complete Summary" chip

### Buttons don't appear
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled
- Refresh the page
- Check browser console for errors

### Wrong merchant data
- Double-check the merchant name spelling
- Try variations (e.g., "711" vs "7-Eleven")
- Some merchants may have multiple name formats

### Rate limit reached
- Wait until tomorrow (resets at midnight)
- The AI will show exactly when it resets
- You can still view past conversations in the sidebar
